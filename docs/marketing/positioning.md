# Ondra positioning and messaging

The landing page copy (`apps/site`) is written from this document. Change the positioning here first, then the page.

## One line

AI agents that answer every call, WhatsApp, text and email a dealership gets, book the visit, and hand the right buyer to the right person. VIPs always reach the owner directly.

## Who we sell to first (in order)

1. **US exporters selling to Africa and the Middle East.** Buyers are on WhatsApp, in other time zones, asking about shipping and payment. Most dealer AI tools don't serve them. This is where we win first, and where the pilot dealer sits.
2. **Dealers serving immigrant communities.** Same WhatsApp behaviour, domestic buyers.
3. **Independent dealers generally**, especially those with no DMS.
4. **Franchised dealers.** Listed on the page, but this is where Numa and Flai are strongest. Don't lead with them.

The buyer is the **owner or GM**. They feel the missed calls and the leads stuck on a salesperson's phone, and they decide.

## The problem, in the dealer's words

- "We miss calls after hours, and when everyone's on the floor."
- "Our WhatsApp is on Daniel's phone. When he's off, nobody answers."
- "Nigeria buyers message at 2am our time. By morning they've asked three other dealers."
- "I don't want a bot talking to my best clients."

## Against competitors

Numa, Flai and similar tools focus on US franchised dealers over phone, text and email. Our differences: WhatsApp-first, cross-border buyers and time zones, works with no DMS, VIP handling built in. **Don't name competitors on the public page.** Use this in sales calls only.

## Claims we must not make yet

The product isn't built yet. Until it is, the page sticks to what the plan commits to and avoids:

- **Numbers:** response times, conversion lifts, "X dealers use Ondra". We have none yet.
- **Testimonials or logos** without written permission. The pilot dealer is described, not named. The mockup's "Mr. Jay Autos" sign was removed for this reason.
- **Named DMS integrations.** Which DMS the pilots use is still an open question (BUILD_PLAN §17.4).
- **Languages the voice agent speaks.** STT/TTS vendors haven't been chosen (§17.3).
- **Prices.** Not set (§17.6).
- **"Live this week."** WhatsApp needs Meta business verification, and production SMS needs A2P 10DLC registration. Both take weeks. Phone and email can be fast.
- **"Indistinguishable from a human."** Don't say it. The page leans the other way: "None of them are people."

## Open questions for the founders

1. **AI disclosure:** does the agent say it's an AI when asked, or at the start of every conversation? The plan is silent. Some states have bot-disclosure laws. Decide this, then add an FAQ answer.
2. **Demo follow-up promise:** the form says "within one business day". Only keep it if someone will actually do that.
3. **Name and domain:** "Ondra" is a working name (§16.1). The domain and contact email are placeholders.

## Getting the first pilots

The landing page doesn't create demand. It converts people you send to it. For the first 10 pilots:

- **Founder-led outreach** to Dallas/Houston/Atlanta/New Jersey exporters, the hubs for Nigeria and Ghana vehicle exports. Use WhatsApp and phone, because that's how these dealers work.
- **Mystery-shop first:** message or call the dealer after hours, record what happens (no answer, a slow reply), then lead the outreach with that. It's the most persuasive demo there is.
- **Referrals from the pilot dealer**, once there's something worth referring.
- **One case study** from the pilot with real numbers (missed calls answered, after-hours leads, bookings). Until it exists, it's the most important marketing asset missing.
